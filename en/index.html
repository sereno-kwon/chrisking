Brand

Chris KingPortland, Oregon · Since 1976

Fit it once,
and it moves with you
to your next bike.

Chris King Precision Components was founded by Chris King in California in 1976. Frustrated by headsets that quickly loosened and rusted, the young machinist cut his own headset with sealed bearings. That headset was Chris King's first product.

Fifty years on, Chris King still makes its headsets, hubs and bottom brackets in its own factory in Portland, Oregon. Machining, bearing production, anodizing, assembly and inspection all happen under one roof. Parts you use for years, service, and move to your next bike. That is what Chris King considers a good part.

Built to last.
Made to be serviced.

Chris King parts are designed for service from the start. Bearings can be taken apart, cleaned and regreased, and wear parts are available separately. That is why Chris King headsets and hubs often outlast the frames they were fitted to. Repairing instead of replacing is the most basic way Chris King practises sustainability.

The factory recycles the aluminium chips from machining and keeps the whole production process in one place, so quality and environmental impact are managed directly. This approach, started in a small Portland factory, is now why riders and frame builders around the world choose Chris King.

Headsets

NoThreadSet · InSet · DropSet10 Year Warranty

Our first product in 1976,
still at the heart
of Chris King.

The headset is the least visible part of a bike, yet one of the first to fail. Rain, sweat and every impact from the road go straight into it. Chris King started with exactly this problem. In-house sealed bearings, precisely machined cups and seals that keep dirt out preserve the same steering feel after tens of thousands of kilometres.

The GripLock design of the NoThreadSet centres the steerer precisely and holds it without play. Choose the InSet for tapered head tubes and the DropSet for integrated frames. Every Chris King headset comes with a 10-year warranty.

03

Hubs

R45 · R45D · BoostRingDrive

Push the pedal
and it engages instantly.
And that sound.

The heart of every Chris King hub is RingDrive. Instead of conventional pawls, two wide toothed rings engage across their full face, spreading the load evenly and transferring power the moment you push the pedal. 45 points of engagement on the R45, 72 on the Boost and Superboost mountain hubs. It is also where the famous Chris King buzz comes from.

The hubs also use in-house bearings and are designed for adjustable preload and full service. That is why many riders keep the same hubs through several wheel rebuilds.

04

Bottom Brackets

ThreadFit 24 · 30 · T4724mm · 30mm Spindle

Where the most force goes,
and where it should be quietest.

The bottom bracket is where a rider's power passes first and hardest. Chris King bottom brackets use the same in-house bearings as our headsets and hubs, turning quietly here for years. Choose ThreadFit 24 or 30 for BSA threaded frames and ThreadFit T47 for T47 frames.

The bearings are designed to be opened and regreased, so a noisy bottom bracket becomes a part you service, not one you throw away. Match the colour to your headset and hubs and the whole bike comes together in one tone.

See it at Rubi Workshop →

ThreadFit 24 · ThreadFit 30

The choice for BSA threaded frames

ThreadFit for BSA threaded frames comes as ThreadFit 24 for 24mm-spindle Shimano cranks and ThreadFit 30 for 30mm-spindle cranks. Precisely machined cups keep the bearings in exact alignment.

Rubi Workshop will help you confirm the right model for your crank and frame.

ThreadFit T47

Threaded and quiet, even on T47

T47 is a threaded standard with a large-diameter shell, giving the room of a wide shell without the creaks of press-fit, and is now common on road and gravel frames. Chris King ThreadFit T47 comes in external (x), internal (i) and asymmetric (A) versions with 24mm or 30mm spindle options to suit your frame and crank.
